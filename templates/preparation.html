Quantitative Aptitude Strategy

Quantitative Aptitude evaluates numerical ability, mathematical comprehension, and calculation speed. Almost every exam (SSC, Banking, RRB, UPSC CSAT) tests this section.

Essential Syllabus Topics:
Percentage, Ratio & Proportion
Profit, Loss & Discount
Time, Speed, Distance & Trains
Time & Work, Pipes & Cisterns
Simple & Compound Interest
Geometry & Mensuration (SSC)
Algebra & Quadratic Equations
Data Interpretation (Pie/Bar/Tables)
Preparation Rule

Memorize multiplication tables up to 30, squares up to 50, cubes up to 30, and fractional percentages (e.g., 1/7 = 14.28%) to solve questions in 30 seconds.

Reasoning Ability & General Intelligence

Divided into Verbal Reasoning (logic, series, coding) and Non-Verbal Reasoning (patterns, paper folding, mirror images).

High-Scoring Topics:
Syllogisms (Venn diagrams)
Coding-Decoding & Letter Series
Blood Relations & Direction Sense
Seating Arrangement & Floor Puzzles
Order, Ranking & Inequality
Statement & Assumptions / Arguments
Banking vs SSC Difference: Banking exams focus heavily on complex time-consuming Puzzles and Seating Arrangements, whereas SSC and Railways emphasize quick analogies, series, and non-verbal patterns.
Recommended Books
  • A Modern Approach to Verbal & Non-Verbal Reasoning
    Author: Dr. R.S. Aggarwal
  • Analytical Reasoning
    Author: M.K. Pandey
    Best for Critical Reasoning & Assumptions.

English Language & Comprehension

Core Pillars:

Reading Comprehension Passages
Synonyms, Antonyms & One-Word Substitutions
Idioms & Phrasal Verbs
Para Jumbles (Sentence Rearrangement)
Active & Passive Voice, Direct & Indirect Speech

Current Affairs Strategy (Last 6–12 Months)

Current events carry heavy weightage in UPSC, SSC, Banking, and State PSC examinations. Focus on government schemes, international summits, RBI monetary policy changes, science and defence advancements, sports awards, and national appointments.

Newspaper Habit

Read editorial and national pages of The Hindu or The Indian Express daily for deep context.

Monthly Compilations

Revise monthly PDF digests (e.g. Yojana, Kurukshetra, or PIB releases) for rapid factual retention.

Government Portals

Refer to pib.gov.in for direct government policy press releases.

Technical Subjects for Engineering & IT Candidates

Preparation guidelines for technical examinations (NIC Scientist B, IBPS SO IT, GATE PSUs, SSC JE, RRB JE).

Computer Science & IT (NIC / IBPS SO / ISRO)
  • Data Structures & Algorithms: Cormen (CLRS) or standard university notes
  • Operating Systems: Galvin (Processes, Threads, Deadlocks, Paging)
  • Database Management (DBMS): Korth or Navathe (SQL, Normalization, ACID)
  • Computer Networks: Tanenbaum / Kurose & Ross (TCP/IP, Routing, DNS)
  • Cybersecurity Basics: Cryptography, firewalls, and network security
Core Engineering (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical)
  • Civil: Building Materials, Soil Mechanics, Structural Analysis, Fluid Mechanics
  • Mechanical: Thermodynamics, Fluid Machinery, Strength of Materials, Theory of Machines
  • Electrical: Power Systems, Electrical Machines, Control Systems, Network Theory
  • Primary Exam Source: Previous 15 years GATE & ESE objective question papers.

Interview & Personality Test Guidance

The interview stage does not re-test your textbook knowledge; it evaluates your character, poise, communication clarity, ethics, and psychological stability.

Civil Services & Banking PO Interviews

- Know every word on your Detailed Application Form (DAF) or bio-data (home district, hobbies, graduation subjects).
- Frame balanced viewpoints on controversial policy matters without taking extreme ideological stances.
- When you do not know a factual answer, politely state: "I do not know the exact details at the moment, sir/ma'am, but I will look into it."

5-Day SSB (Services Selection Board)

- Assesses 15 Officer Like Qualities (OLQ) across Effective Intelligence, Social Adaptability, Cooperation, and Stamina.
- Maintain natural authenticity during Psychological tests (TAT/WAT/SRT); do not try to give pre-coached artificial answers.
- During Group Discussions and Ground Obstacles (GTO), contribute constructively to team success rather than attempting to dominate.
