Central Ministries

Staff Selection Commission (SSC)

India's major recruitment body for Group B & C civil administrative cadres across central ministries and departments.

  • SSC CGL: Graduate level (ASO, Income Tax, GST Inspector)
  • SSC CHSL: 12th pass (LDC, JSA, Data Entry Operator)
  • SSC MTS: 10th pass general central service
  • SSC CPO: Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police & CAPFs
ssc.gov.in
Indian Railways

Indian Railways (RRB & RRC)

Technical, operational, and non-technical popular categories across 21 regional Railway Recruitment Boards.

  • RRB NTPC: Station Master, Goods Guard, Clerks (12th & Degree)
  • RRB ALP & Technician: Matric + ITI or Diploma/B.Tech
  • RRB JE: Junior Engineer across civil, electrical, mechanical
  • RRC Level 1: Track maintainer and assistant roles (10th/ITI)
indianrailways.gov.in
Public Finance

Banking & Financial Institutions

Nationalized public sector banks (PSBs) and apex regulatory institutions with swift annual recruitment cycles.

  • IBPS & SBI PO: Probationary Officers (Any Degree, 20–30 yrs)
  • IBPS SO (IT Officer): B.Tech CSE/IT, MCA (Scale 1 Specialist)
  • IBPS & SBI Clerk: Customer support and clerical cadre
  • RBI Grade B: Apex central bank officer recruitment

All-India Services

UPSC & Civil Services

India's most prestigious competitive examinations for high administrative, police, diplomatic, and engineering posts.

  • Civil Services Exam (CSE): IAS, IPS, IFS, IRS (Any Graduation)
  • Engineering Services (ESE): Class-1 engineers in Railways, CPWD
  • CDS & NDA: Commissioned Armed Forces leadership
  • CAPF (AC): Assistant Commandants in BSF, CRPF, CISF

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) Career Roadmap
1. Educational Eligibility

- SSC CGL: Bachelor’s Degree in any discipline from a recognized university. Age 18–30 or 18–32 years (post-dependent).
- SSC CHSL: 12th standard pass. Age 18–27 years.
- SSC JE: Diploma or Degree in Civil, Mechanical, or Electrical Engineering. Age up to 30/32 years.
- SSC MTS: 10th standard pass. Age 18–25 or 18–27 years.

2. Common Selection Stages

- Tier-I (Computer Based Test): 100 questions (200 marks) testing General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, and English Comprehension. Qualifying in nature.
- Tier-II (Mains CBT): Mathematical Abilities, Reasoning, English Language, General Awareness, Computer Knowledge Module, and Data Entry Speed Test (DEST).
- Stage 3: Document Verification (DV) conducted directly by user departments.

3. Key Preparation Topics

Arithmetic, Advanced Math (Geometry, Trigonometry, Algebra), Non-verbal Reasoning, Current Affairs of past 6 months, English Grammar & Vocabulary.

Official Link: Apply and check schedules at ssc.gov.in.

Indian Railways (RRB) Selection Guide
1. Educational Norms

- NTPC Graduate: Any Bachelor’s Degree. Roles: Station Master, Goods Train Manager. Age: 18–33/36.
- NTPC Undergraduate: 12th Pass. Roles: Junior Clerk, Accounts Clerk, Trains Clerk. Age: 18–30.
- ALP (Assistant Loco Pilot): 10th + ITI, or 3-year Diploma in Mechanical/Electrical/Automobile Engineering, or B.Tech.
- Junior Engineer: 3-year Diploma or Degree in relevant engineering branch.

2. Selection Stages

- CBT 1: Screening test on Mathematics, General Intelligence, General Science, and General Awareness.
- CBT 2: In-depth examination determining merit.
- CBAT (Aptitude Test): Mandatory only for Station Master & Loco Pilot posts.
- Medical Standard A-1: Strict distant visual acuity 6/6 without glasses for Loco Pilot posts.

Official Source: Regional RRB sites such as rrbcdg.gov.in.

Public Sector Banking Roadmap
1. Eligibility Criteria

- PO / Clerk: Graduation in any discipline from a recognized University. Age: 20–30 years for PO, 20–28 years for Clerk.
- Specialist Officer (IT): 4-year Engineering Degree in Computer Science, Computer Applications, Information Technology, or Electronics. Age: 20–30 years.

2. Fast Selection Stages (6-8 Months Total)

- Preliminary Exam: English Language, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning Ability (60 minutes sectional timer).
- Mains Exam: Data Analysis & Interpretation, General Economy & Banking Awareness, Reasoning & Computer Aptitude, plus English Descriptive Essay/Letter.
- Interview: Panel interview (for PO and SO cadres only; Clerical posts do not have interviews).

Official Portal: ibps.in and sbi.co.in/careers.

UPSC Civil Services Examination (CSE) Guide
1. Eligibility Criteria

Degree in any discipline (B.A., B.Sc., B.Com., B.Tech., MBBS). Age: 21–32 years for General category (relaxations: 3 years OBC, 5 years SC/ST). Attempts: 6 for General, 9 for OBC, unlimited for SC/ST within age limits.

2. Three Rigorous Stages

- Preliminary Examination: Paper I (General Studies - 200 marks determines cutoff) + Paper II (CSAT - 33% qualifying mark).
- Mains Examination (Descriptive): 9 papers totaling 1750 marks (2 Language papers, 1 Essay, 4 GS papers, 2 Optional subject papers).
- Personality Test: 275 marks interview assessing leadership, intellectual integrity, and administrative balance.

Official Commission Portal: upsc.gov.in.

Maharashtra Public Services (MPSC) Guide
1. State Specific Requirements

- Domicile: Maharashtra Domicile certificate mandatory for state reservation quotas.
- Language: Fluency in Marathi (Reading, Writing, Speaking) is tested in qualifying language papers.

2. Major Examinations

- MPSC Rajyaseva: State civil services for Deputy Collector, DySP, Tehsildar.
- MPSC Non-Gazetted Group B & C: Police Sub-Inspector (PSI), State Tax Inspector (STI), Assistant Section Officer (ASO).
- Talathi / Zilla Parishad Bharti: Direct recruitments for revenue and rural governance.

Official Portal: mpsc.gov.in.

Police & CAPF Services Guide
1. Physical Standards (Mandatory)

Male candidates typically require minimum height of 170 cm (relaxable for hill areas/ST), chest expansion 80-85 cm. Female candidates minimum height 157 cm. Running standards (e.g. 5 km in 24 mins or 1600 m in 6.5 mins).

2. Selection Stages

- Written Examination (General Intelligence, Reasoning, Numerical Ability, English/Hindi).
- Physical Standard Test (PST) and Physical Endurance Test (PET).
- Detailed Medical Examination (DME).

Official Links: SSC website for CPO/GD at ssc.gov.in.

Armed Forces Commissioning Guide
1. Key Entry Schemes

- NDA & NA: 12th pass (PCM for Navy/Air Force). Age 16.5–19.5.
- CDS (Combined Defence Services): Graduates for Army, Navy, Air Force academies.
- AFCAT: Air Force Common Admission Test for Flying and Ground Duty branches.
- Technical Entries: Direct SSB interviews for B.Tech graduates (TGC, SSC-Tech).

2. 5-Day SSB Interview

Stage 1: OIR Test & PPDT (Screening). Stage 2: Psychological tests (TAT, WAT, SRT), Group Testing Officer (GTO) tasks, Personal Interview, and Final Board Conference.

Official Portals: joinindianarmy.nic.in, joinindiannavy.gov.in, careerindianairforce.cdac.in.

Teaching & Academic Cadres Guide
1. Necessary Teacher Certifications

- PRT (Primary Teacher): 12th + D.El.Ed/JBT + CTET Paper-I.
- TGT (Trained Graduate Teacher): Bachelor's Degree + B.Ed + CTET Paper-II.

Public Sector Undertakings & Technical Research
1. Gate Score vs Independent CBT

- GATE Route: Major Maharatnas (IOCL, NTPC, ONGC, PowerGrid, BHEL) shortlist based on GATE percentile followed by GD/Interview.
- Independent Recruitment Exams: ISRO (ICRB), DRDO (RAC), NIELIT, NIC (Scientist B), BEL, and CDAC conduct independent all-India CBT exams focusing on core engineering fundamentals.

2. Computer Science & IT Specialization

NIC Scientist B and IBPS SO IT Officer test Data Structures, Algorithms, Compiler Design, Database Management Systems, Computer Networks, and Cybersecurity.

Official Portal: gate2025.iitr.ac.in and nielit.in.
